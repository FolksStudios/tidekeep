Tidekeep: Citadel of the Realm

The tactile, physics-driven castle-building and siege defense simulation. Combining intuitive 0.5m grid masonry with a deterministic real-time Jolt 3D statics graph, dynamic moat hydraulics, and autonomous Stronghold-style colony life.

Masterful Architecture

Engineered for strategists, master builders, and aesthetic enthusiasts alike.

🏰

Tactile Masonry & Statics

Every stone block bears physical load. A deterministic real-time support graph calculates structural stability. Knock out a foundation pillar, and towers collapse into simulated Jolt-physics rubble.

🌊

Moat Hydraulics & Gates

Excavate working water channels into the flood plains and watch rushing turquoise river water flood your moats. Lock the heavy iron drawbridge with a single tap for +2000 shield HP.

⚔️

High-Stakes Sieges

Defend the parapets against armored battering rams, catapults, and subterranean sappers. Visual telegraphing highlights endangered stone blocks seconds before impact.

👥

Living Medieval Colony

Masons haul stone from the quarry; bakers carry bread from mills to the granary; armored guards patrol the courtyards. Citizens automatically form bucket brigades when fire breaks out.

🕊️

Peaceful Zen Sandbox

Infinite stone, zero siege pressure, and full control over sun angle, weather, and snow coverage. Capture breathtaking wallpaper dioramas with the built-in tilt-shift photo mode.

📜

16-Character Sharing

Share and import magnificent fortress creations worldwide with friends using compact 16-character codes or illustrated parchment postcards—completely offline without user accounts.
